= RTDB structural redesign

Six guarantees could not be closed in the inherited architecture.
Three independent designs converged on the replacement below.
This document is the target contract for the redesign increments.
The second review's Multi serving and custody boundaries extend that design.

== Owner decisions

* A client-supplied system time may equal or follow the preceding system time and cannot lead the authenticated source-log append observation for the original request (tolerance zero).
* An await token establishes a causal lower bound.
  A system-time cap filters the chosen prefix, and a fresh admission can include later corrections below that cap.
  An issued exact snapshot token identifies the same applied cut on every successful re-admission under current authorization, retention and custody.
  A placed SQL token seals the original owner, partition position, physical generation, historical cap and clock interpretation.
  A reopened node can serve the identical installed cut or reconstruct it from the sealed checkpoint and its protected replica suffix.
  A genesis cut requires the retained replica range from the initial position.
  Reconstruction retains journal custody through answer release and requires the exact target tail and data head.
  An advanced head with the same historical timestamp never substitutes for the captured position.
* A view admitted before an erasure keeps its answers until the erasure deadline; afterwards a read that needs destroyed key material fails with `rtdb/erased-under-view`.
  SNAP-1 and DUR-1 are amended accordingly.
* An erasure reaches forks that retain shared custody of that history.
  An independent export or archive is re-encrypted and governed by its own custody authority.
* Transaction user metadata is retained only for the retry-receipt window, and is erasable with its unit before then.
* XTDB wire, configuration and storage compatibility is not a constraint.

== Coordinates

A decision's identity and serialization order use its authenticated history, partition and position.
Operation ordinals preserve the original order within each decision.
Temporal SQL system time is nondecreasing and may tie.
An automatic assignment uses the maximum of the previous system time and the original append observation, or the append observation at genesis.
A corrected external hint retains its provenance and follows the same explicit bound if admitted as system time.
Resolve SQL precedence inside a selected positional cut, filtering by system and valid time, and resolve equal system times by position and ordinal.
Equal-time overwritten events remain identifiable at their positional cuts.
A superseded equal-time event has no positive-length SQL system-time interval.
The installed clock authority independently samples a durable monotone frontier, including while application histories are idle.
A successor preserves its clock domain's frontier and fences the old authority.
Live judgments require freshly certified evidence in the correct domain.
The receiver binds its decision observation to the original predecessor, attempt, temporal terms, effects and outcome at the ordering step.
Replay preserves that evidence.
SQL CURRENT_TIMESTAMP uses the recorded query or decision clock.
Physical acceptance requires certified bounds and timely original evidence from the journal's promised event.
A copied record and a subsequent clock reading carry no original vote.
Catalog admission uses a monotone floor recorded by the residence ledger.
Physical reclamation requires a grant from the storage owner.
Expiry closes issuance. Retained operations keep their dependencies until drain or an enforced bound through their last possible access.
Receipt deadlines use the installed decision clock.
Erasure starts at publication of the erasing generation and uses the custody authority's bounded grant clock.

== Reclamation and recovery guarantees

GC-1::
An admitted read or fork holds every physical publication reachable from its admitted scope at the original basis for the promised lifetime, subject to ordered erasure.
In-flight operations keep their dependencies through their final use and provider drain.
A published recovery root holds its complete dependencies and required replay suffix.
A packed member holds the physical pack that supplies it.

GC-2::
Only the physical owner may grant deletion of an immutable provider publication.
In one ordered decision it consumes retirement evidence, establishes that the publication has no eligible dependencies, and permanently excludes new references or a late publication that could re-establish the dependency.
The grant names the exact provider version or terminal creation identity and remains valid through retry and owner replacement.
The owner keeps a publication in custody whenever a dependency lacks exact replacement evidence or future use lacks exact exclusion evidence. An incomplete inventory leaves the affected scope in custody.

DUR-2::
Every selectable recovery path authenticates a complete checkpoint and sufficient committed replay under the current recovery authority.
Replacing a path preserves reconstruction of the acknowledged history within the supported fault model and fences superseded dependencies.
A pointer in a fenced journal range confers no access to a detached payload, including when that journal returns or is restored.

LIVE-2::
Each retired physical publication becomes eligible after its last dependency drains and future use is fenced.
Fair collection and eventual provider settlement or conclusive absence reclaim it while unrelated holds survive or excluded historical journals are unavailable.
A publication awaiting exact replacement or exclusion evidence stays in custody. The collection obligation starts once the evidence establishes eligibility.
Media disposal has independent evidence.

=== Certified decisions and fresh reads

A placed append receipt names an exact decided prefix of the authoritative history.
The fenced writer obtains durable payload votes, then installs the decision certificate on a quorum of distinct physical journals in the active configuration before releasing the append receipt.
Each vote binds the original configuration, journal and exact digest of the recoverable prefix.
A certificate binds the certified predecessor and the admission evidence for the suffix.
A synchronous transaction reply also requires application of the exact batch and outcome.
Provisional intake receipts only name an attempt.

A fresh read probes the active configuration with a fresh invocation nonce and selects the greatest compatible installed certificate from a quorum.
It covers every cut installed on a quorum before invocation, including when a reply was lost, and every earlier completed fresh read in the same history.
If its probe or a supplied proof establishes quorum installation, capture may proceed.
Otherwise the reader writes back the same certificate to a quorum, accepting an attested extension while keeping the selected cut fixed.
Uncertified staged bytes above the selection add no read dependency.

Holder and subscriber use the same freshness contract.
An enrolled cold reader of an ordered placement acquires and renews a serving lease against its exact retained replay hold, independently of the writer grant.
Ordinary Flight release checks durable range evidence and point and control equalities on a protected applied cut, under the original history and deadline.
The serving attachment acquires protection for the original checkpoint and suffix, waits for same-history application and captures catalog and data at the selected cut.
Freshness is retryably unavailable when authority, quorum installation, custody or exact application cannot be established.
With a stable active quorum, recoverable data and sufficient apply and query service, a reader can serve without the writer or silent journals.
Gateway routing may refresh and retry a replay-safe unpinned read within the same history before output, preserving its audience and causal constraints.
A transaction or explicit basis keeps its original cut.

The predecessor seals on its own quorum, fencing old votes and returning durable decisions in the same local order.
Recovery preserves compatible certificates and potentially chosen ordinary values from sealed accepted evidence.
Before activation a successor quorum installs the recovered prefix and its usable recovery closure.

=== Ranked cutoff at ties

The existing cutoff uses a strict score bound and keeps all ties at the boundary.
This preserves SQL results but may require scanning most postings when many documents have equal scores.
Document-local scoring also removes document length from the rank key, so term-frequency ties deserve explicit coverage.

Extend early termination only where the planner can prove a complete SQL ordering key and the search file can supply a conservative bound for that key.
The bound combines each window's upper score with its earliest possible secondary key under the SQL comparator, including direction, null ordering and collation.
Resident and persisted candidates share that comparator.
The native index must encode a usable secondary key for the admitted ordering; an IID's hash order can bound a SQL ID only where the representation preserves that order.
A window with an equal or unknown bound remains eligible for examination.
The rank proof must cover each unread window, including those whose score ties the last retained row.
Tests compare an exhaustive scan with the pruned plan over tied scores, across partitions, and with offset and limit.

The five-argument score uses the caller's corpus observations and the declared stored-length statistic.
A corpus computed with an ordinary SQL aggregate pays for its selected rows.
A future precomputed corpus would need an exact visibility and temporal scope before a query could reuse it.
