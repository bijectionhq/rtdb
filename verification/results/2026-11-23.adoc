= RTDB kernel study results, 2026-11-23

== W1 · Successor trie collector and lease expiry

The collector contract in link:../../dev/doc/gc.allium[gc.allium] starts a new wait after a collector restarts or takes over.
The study covers a newly selected trie under that contract.
It compares an observation scoped to one collector with a weaker design that permits a predecessor's observation to carry over.

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
|Evidence |Result

|TLC 2026.11.19.180144
|The baseline explored 9,792 distinct states across two terms, one file and one reader, with a time horizon of nine ticks, a four-tick lease and origins in {0, 9}.
It checked that collection follows a full wait measured by the current collector and that an admitted provider read retains its file while in flight.
With weak fairness of collection, a successor with an elapsed window eventually collects.
The liveness premise requires time to reach the end of that window within the modeled horizon.

|Controls and witness
|The abstract observation reuse control violates the own-observation invariant and the I/O-retention invariant when checked separately.
The restart-window control violates the temporal progress property.
The reachability witness observes collection in a successor with a different clock origin.

|Native Rust
|The production collector runs twice as distinct instances over the same in-memory store and partition.
For a 500-microsecond lease, the successor keeps the data and metadata on first observation and at 499 microseconds, then deletes both and publishes the retirement exactly at 500 microseconds.
The progress control resets the observation window on each collection pass and fails at the final retirement assertion.
The full maintenance crate passed all 53 cases, including 12 collection integration cases.
|===

The model assumes clocks with equal rates and arbitrary origins within the finite set.
The reader anchor comes from a confirmed prefix that predates retirement.
I/O finishes within the modeled two-tick bound, which equals the lease margin.
The existing link:../verus/read_lease/laws.rs[Verus lease laws] cover the elapsed-time inequalities for bounded drift when the margin also covers provider execution.
Provider duration, the scheduling of real elections, and reclamation through resumed intents and Multi's deferred reaper remain separate obligations.
The native fixture exercises collector replacement with an eligible catalog and manual clocks.

The maintained runner is link:../run.py[verification/run.py].
The selected cases use link:../tla/CollectorTurnover.cfg[CollectorTurnover.cfg] and the native replay `a_replacement_collector_waits_a_whole_lease_on_its_own_clock`.
