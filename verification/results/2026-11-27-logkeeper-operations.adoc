== Automatic recovery after two voters are lost

RTDB was built from `498b15597a7629dcc4a5c2b62391c837bdffa9fa` with the `multi` feature and three documentation-only additions required by the vendored SQL parser's lint.
RTDB main subsequently landed those missing comments independently.
The keeper processes used explicit `fsync` mode for this functional test.

The fixture had three voters, a single catalog keeper, and two spare keepers in the failure domains of the voters to be lost.
RTDB acknowledged 2,014 transactions before it was stopped.
SIGHUP caused a keeper to reload its credentials while RTDB was running.
The test stopped both lost voters, stopped the survivor, and ran `logkeeper keeper recover-stream` on the survivor for the RTDB catalog and database streams.
Each command named both stopped voters and both replacements.
After starting the survivor and RTDB, RTDB logged `rtdb/journal-recovered` for both streams and resumed without manual acknowledgement.
TxStatus returned all 2,014 pre-recovery receipts and 2,502 receipts for new commits after recovery.

== Monitoring overhead

Eight paired 10-second measurements on an Apple M1 compared `929b46d` with `6de4378` using plain fsync, 128 active streams, and batches of 16 records.
Median throughput was 72,792 versus 72,152 records/s, a decrease of 0.88%.
Three idle full-sync pairs put both medians at 0.34 CPU cores.
The loaded full-sync throughput comparison remains inconclusive.

These local tests exercise the keeper upgrade and RTDB recovery paths.
The hosted-cell cutover, independent failure-domain measurements, and a completed long soak remain separate qualification steps.
